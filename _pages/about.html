About

I work at the Computational Engineering and Robotics Lab at Carnegie Mellon University under Kenji Shimada, where my research sits at the intersection of perception and planning: teaching robots to decide where to look in order to inspect a part completely and efficiently. Day to day that means coverage viewpoint planning, pose estimation, point cloud registration, and a good deal of sensor and hardware integration.

Before starting the PhD I completed my Master's at CMU in robotics and controls, with a thesis on learning-based coverage viewpoint planning. I like problems where the algorithm has to survive contact with a real machine. If you are working on something in that space, I would be glad to hear about it — get in touch.
