Four years with DJS Racing, the Formula Student team at the University of Mumbai — as Structural Head, Finance Lead, and eventually the team's inspection and test driver.

What is Formula Student?

Formula Student is an international engineering competition where student teams design, build, test, and race small formula-style racing cars. Teams are judged on engineering design, cost and manufacturing analysis, business presentation, and dynamic events — acceleration, skid pad, autocross, and endurance.

Participants gain hands-on experience in industrial engineering practice, project management, manufacturing, and team collaboration, which is what makes it such a rewarding challenge for aspiring engineers.

DJS Racing

DJS Racing is a Formula Student team under the University of Mumbai. Since its founding the team has established itself as one of the best nationally, winning accolades at national and international events and setting a benchmark in the static events. Alumni of the team often go on to serve as judges in later competitions.

Since 2020 the team has shifted from combustion vehicles to the Electric and Autonomous category. Their first EV debuted at Formula Bharat 2022.

Structural Head

I joined as a junior member of the chassis team and was later promoted to Structural Head. In that capacity I oversaw the shift from a combustion vehicle to an electric design, headed the production of two chassis, and worked on the design and manufacture of our aerodynamic package.

Under my leadership DJS Racing successfully defended its cost budget and cost design for two years, placing in the top five. I led the design and prototyping of a modular firewall and an in-house carbon fibre impact attenuator, both since patented by the university, and published a paper on efficient use of carbon fibre in my final year.

Finance Lead

I joined the B-team of the Business Plan group in my first year. Financial modelling and presentation design helped the B-team secure a national rank of fourth; the financial model I developed and defended for our "firm" earned an honourable mention from the judges.

I was subsequently put in charge of the Business Plan team's finances and served as co-lead for the event. During that stint we placed second nationally, narrowly beaten by IIT Bombay. The financial templates I built then are still in use by the team.

Inspection and Test Driver

Having been an active participant in the design and manufacture of two cars, I became the inspection and testing driver in my final year. I demonstrated driver safety during Vehicle Inspection and gained approval for all track events.

The team cleared the entire Vehicle Inspection in record time that year. Under particular scrutiny were the modular firewall, which allowed easy disassembly for accessing the accumulator, and our in-house carbon fibre impact attenuator, which protects the driver in a frontal crash.
