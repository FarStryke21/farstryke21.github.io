Overview

Developed as part of a semester-long mechatronics capstone, the BlackFlag utility robot is a solution for operational support on large marine vessels. The objective was a system robust enough to conduct routine inspections and execute intricate operations aboard cargo vessels while withstanding the harsh marine environment.

The robot pairs a 5-degree-of-freedom revolute arm with a mobile base and a custom perception package. As a member of the five-person team, I led the Perception and Sensing Subsystem, overseeing software integration and the final deployment of the working system.
